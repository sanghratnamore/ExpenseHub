# ExpenseHub — Project Vision

## 1. Project Name

**ExpenseHub**

**Tagline:** Manage money together, effortlessly.

---

## 2. Project Overview

ExpenseHub is a collaborative expense management platform designed to help individuals and groups organize, track, split, and settle their expenses in one place.

The platform is primarily intended for college students, friends, roommates, and young professionals who frequently share expenses during trips, shared living arrangements, and group activities.

---

## 3. Problem Statement

Managing shared expenses can become complicated when multiple people contribute different amounts toward common activities.

For example, during a group trip, one person may pay for accommodation, another may pay for transportation, and others may pay for food or activities. At the end of the trip, manually calculating who owes whom can be time-consuming and confusing.

Similarly, people living together may have to manage rent, electricity, internet, groceries, maintenance, and other recurring expenses paid by different members.

Individuals may also struggle to understand their own spending habits when personal expenses are recorded across different applications, notes, spreadsheets, or messages.

ExpenseHub aims to address these problems by providing a centralized platform for personal and shared expense management.

---

## 4. Proposed Solution

ExpenseHub will provide a single platform where users can:

* Track personal income and expenses.
* Create groups for shared expenses.
* Invite other users into groups.
* Record expenses and identify who paid.
* Split expenses among selected members.
* Calculate balances automatically.
* Record settlements between members.
* Monitor spending through dashboards and reports.
* Manage expenses related to trips and shared living arrangements.

---

## 5. Target Users

### Primary Users

* College students
* Friends and travel groups
* Roommates
* Young professionals

### Potential Future Users

* Couples
* Families
* Small community groups

---

## 6. Core Modules

### Personal Expense Management

Users can record and categorize their personal expenses and view summaries of their spending.

### Group Expense Management

Users can create groups, invite members, record shared expenses, and calculate individual balances.

### Trip Management

Users can create trips and manage expenses associated with a particular trip.

### Shared Living Management

Users living together can manage recurring household expenses such as rent, electricity, internet, groceries, and maintenance.

### Settlement Management

The system can show outstanding balances and allow users to record completed settlements.

### Dashboard and Reports

Users can view summaries, spending patterns, and group expense information.

---

## 7. Project Goals

The primary goals of ExpenseHub are:

1. Reduce the manual effort required to calculate shared expenses.
2. Provide transparency among members of an expense group.
3. Make personal expense tracking simple.
4. Centralize trip and shared-living expenses.
5. Provide useful summaries of spending.
6. Develop a practical software system using modern development practices.
7. Provide a learning platform for the development team to gain experience in software engineering.

---

## 8. Project Differentiation

ExpenseHub is not intended to claim that expense splitting is a completely new concept.

Its differentiation is the combination of personal expense tracking, group expense management, trip expenses, and shared-living expenses within a single platform.

The initial target audience will focus on students, friends, roommates, and young professionals.

---

## 9. Initial MVP Scope

The first version of ExpenseHub will focus on:

* User registration and login
* User profiles
* Personal expense management
* Expense categories
* Group creation
* Group membership
* Expense recording
* Equal expense splitting
* Custom expense splitting
* Balance calculation
* Settlement recording
* Basic dashboards
* Basic reports

Advanced features will only be considered after the MVP is stable.

---

## 10. Out of Scope for MVP

The initial version will not include:

* Bank account integration
* UPI payment integration
* Investment management
* Loan management
* Credit score management
* Tax management
* Cryptocurrency
* Automated financial advice

---

## 11. Project Vision

> **To simplify the way people manage money together by providing a transparent, centralized, and easy-to-use platform for personal and shared expenses.**

---

## 12. Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful if a group of users can:

1. Create accounts.
2. Create a group.
3. Invite members.
4. Record shared expenses.
5. Split expenses among members.
6. Automatically view individual balances.
7. Record settlements.
8. View personal and group spending summaries.

The system should perform these tasks reliably without requiring users to manually calculate balances using spreadsheets or external tools.
